Selected Research Projects

ECHO — Explainable Co-editing for Presentation Slide Refinement First Author · ACM ICMI 2026

A multimodal slide co-editing system that turns natural-language requests into inspectable, schema-constrained edit plans while preserving meaningful user control.

  • Interaction Architecture: Designed a Plan–Confirm–Execute workflow that grounds requests in selected slide objects, exposes proposed edits for confirmation, and supports rollback.
  • Adaptive AI Pipeline: Implemented stable object grounding, adaptive LLM/VLM routing, schema-constrained planning, and style memory for consistent refinement across slides.
  • Evaluation: Built the CoEdit-Eval benchmark and conducted a study with 14 participants; task-completion time decreased by 25.8% and NASA-TLX workload by 20.8% versus the baseline.
AICo & DefenseMitigator — AI Counseling Systems UIST 2026 Poster · CHI 2027 Submission

Interactive counseling systems that combine adaptive AI guidance with expert oversight to support disclosure while preserving client autonomy.

  • DefenseMitigator: Contributed as third author to a system using a psychological-defense knowledge graph and BDI model for adaptive, evidence-grounded guidance.
  • Formative Research: Conducted and synthesized studies with 6 counselors and 4 clients, translating findings into interaction requirements for autonomy-preserving AI support.
  • AICo Deployment: Extended the work to expert-supervised, multi-client collaboration. A three-week deployment involved 6 licensed psychologists, each overseeing 5 parallel client conversations and reviewing, revising, or overriding AI responses.
MathTrace — Process-Aware Analysis of Mathematical Reasoning AI for Education · HCI

An interaction and learning-analytics project that studies how process traces can reveal mathematical problem-solving strategies beyond final-answer correctness.

  • Study Design: Co-designed pilot studies and analyzed timestamped smart-pen traces, including pauses, revisions, backtracking, and transitions between scratch work and formal solutions.
  • Diagnostic Interface: Developed a replay-and-diagnosis dashboard concept linking handwriting events to step-level evidence and feedback.
  • Research Goal: Explored process-aware learner modeling and evaluation methods for AI tutors that respond to how students reason, not only whether an answer is correct.
AgentMV — State-Guided Multi-Agent Music Video Generation Fifth Author · AI4Art @ ECCV 2026

A long-horizon generation framework that coordinates specialist agents through a Structured Persistent State (SPS).

  • Multi-Agent Coordination: Contributed to a framework coordinating agents for understanding, script planning, visual-asset creation, segment generation, and final assembly.
  • Persistent State: Used SPS to maintain shared context, decisions, and generation progress across the long production pipeline.
  • Publication: Accepted at AI4Art @ ECCV 2026 and forthcoming in the ECCV proceedings.
CassMem — Knowledge-Graph Memory for Long-Term AI Agents Core Researcher · Submitted to WWW 2027

A Cassandra-backed graph-memory system for long-term agents, combining structured and raw memory representations with hybrid retrieval.

  • Memory Design: Developed and profiled graph memory that combines structured/raw representations, hybrid BM25 and dense retrieval, and version-aware readiness checks.
  • Retrieval Evaluation: Evaluated 1,146 held-out LoCoMo queries; Z-score fusion achieved MRR@10 of 0.550 versus 0.523 for compact RRF fusion.
  • Systems Focus: Investigated durable, inspectable memory infrastructure for adaptive agents operating across long interaction histories.
